AI drafting of note-based answers (optional)

Without AI, the extension fills everything that is stored as data in the chart (names, dates, diagnoses with codes, medications, allergies, vitals, contacts) and leaves note-based answers blank. With AI, a de-identified copy of the chart (no names, IDs, addresses, phone numbers, or real dates) is sent to the AI you choose to draft those answers.
