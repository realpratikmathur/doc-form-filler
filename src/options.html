AI drafting of note-based answers (optional)

Without AI, the extension fills everything that is stored as data in the chart (names, dates, diagnoses with codes, medications, allergies, vitals, contacts) and leaves note-based answers blank. With AI, a de-identified copy of the chart (no names, IDs, addresses, phone numbers, or real dates) is sent to Anthropic's Claude to draft those answers. Use an Anthropic account covered by a HIPAA Business Associate Agreement before using real patient charts.

Create one at console.anthropic.com → API keys. Stored only in this Chrome profile.
